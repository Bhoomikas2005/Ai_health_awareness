🚀 HOW TO ACCESS MEDICAL IMAGE ANALYSIS:
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. OPEN FRONTEND:
   URL: http://localhost:5173

2. NAVIGATE TO DISEASE PREDICTION:
   Option A: Click navbar "Features" dropdown → "Disease Prediction"
   Option B: Click home page "🩺 Disease Prediction" card
   Option C: Go directly to: /disease-prediction

3. SELECT IMAGE ANALYSIS MODE:
   Click the "📸 Medical Image Analysis" button at the top

4. UPLOAD MEDICAL IMAGE:
   1. Drag-and-drop OR click to upload
   2. Select image file: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, TIFF
   3. Max size: 10MB
   4. Preview displays automatically

5. ANALYZE IMAGE:
   1. Click "🔬 Analyze Image" button
   2. Wait 1-2 seconds for analysis
   3. Review comprehensive medical report
