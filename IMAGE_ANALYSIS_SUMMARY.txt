📊 DISEASE DETECTION CAPABILITIES
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The system can analyze and detect patterns consistent with:

1. BONE FRACTURE
   📍 Visible in: X-ray, CT scan
   🔍 Patterns: Dark areas, bone breaks
   💯 Confidence: 75-90%
   ⚠️ Severity: HIGH

2. PNEUMONIA
   📍 Visible in: Chest X-ray
   🔍 Patterns: Gray infiltration, consolidation
   💯 Confidence: 70-85%
   ⚠️ Severity: HIGH

3. ARTHRITIS
   📍 Visible in: X-ray, MRI
   🔍 Patterns: Joint degeneration, bone density
   💯 Confidence: 65-80%
   ⚠️ Severity: MEDIUM

4. TUMOR/MASS
   📍 Visible in: CT, MRI, PET scan
   🔍 Patterns: Abnormal density, dark regions
   💯 Confidence: 75-90%
   ⚠️ Severity: CRITICAL

5. SOFT TISSUE INFLAMMATION
   📍 Visible in: MRI, Ultrasound
   🔍 Patterns: Signal changes, swelling
   💯 Confidence: 65-85%
   ⚠️ Severity: MEDIUM

6. DERMATOLOGICAL CONDITIONS
   📍 Visible in: General images, Dermoscopy
   🔍 Patterns: Color changes, swelling, rash
   💯 Confidence: 70-85%
   ⚠️ Severity: LOW

🚀 HOW TO USE
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. OPEN APPLICATION
   ▸ Frontend: http://localhost:5173
   ▸ Backend: http://localhost:5000 (running)

2. NAVIGATE TO DISEASE PREDICTION
   ▸ Click "Features" dropdown in navbar
   ▸ Select "Disease Prediction"
   ▸ OR go directly to: /disease-prediction

3. SELECT IMAGE ANALYSIS MODE
   ▸ Click "📸 Medical Image Analysis" button
   ▸ Leave "🏥 Symptom Analysis" is toggled

4. UPLOAD MEDICAL IMAGE
   ▸ Click upload area OR drag and drop
   ▸ Select file: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, TIFF
   ▸ File size: Max 10MB
   ▸ Preview automatically appears

5. ANALYZE IMAGE
   ▸ Click "🔬 Analyze Image" button
   ▸ Wait for analysis (typically < 2 seconds)
   ▸ Review comprehensive medical report

6. REVIEW RESULTS
   ▸ Image Analysis Details (pixel & color analysis)
   ▸ Risk Assessment (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
   ▸ Disease Predictions (top 3 with confidence)
   ▸ Treatment Options & Recommendations
   ▸ Diagnostic Tests Required
   ▸ Recovery Timeline
