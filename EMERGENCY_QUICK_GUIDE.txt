# Emergency Alert System - Quick Reference Guide

## ⚠️ What Triggers an Emergency Alert?

The emergency alert automatically activates when your health risk score **exceeds 75** (Critical Level).

```
Risk Score > 75 = CRITICAL ALERT 🚨
```

## 🚨 What Happens When Emergency Alert Triggers?

1. **Alert Modal Appears** - Large red popup appears on screen
2. **Cannot Be Ignored** - Cannot interact with other page elements
3. **Location Permission** - Browser asks for your location
4. **Hospitals Found** - Nearby medical facilities are located
5. **Notifications Sent** - 3 nearest hospitals are notified of your emergency

## 📍 Features in Emergency Modal

### Emergency Actions (3 Quick Buttons)

#### 1️⃣ Find Nearby Hospitals
- Locates hospitals within 5 km of your location
- Shows:
  - Hospital name
  - Distance from you
  - Address and phone
  - "Get Directions" button
- Opens Google Maps for navigation

#### 2️⃣ Call 911
- Initiates emergency call
- Connects directly to emergency services
- Available 24/7

#### 3️⃣ Open Google Maps
- Opens full Google Maps in new window
- Can search for hospitals manually
- Shows traffic and routes

### Hospital List Information

Each hospital shows:
- **Name**: Official hospital name
- **Distance**: How far away (in km)
- **Address**: Street address and location
- **Phone**: Direct hospital number
- **Get Directions**: Link to Google Maps navigation

## 🗺️ How Google Maps Integration Works

1. **Your Location**: Shows on map as blue dot
2. **Hospitals**: Red/orange markers with hospital icons
3. **Navigation**: Click hospital → opens Google Maps with directions
4. **Real-time Info**: Traffic updates, alternative routes

## 📱 Using on Mobile vs Desktop

### Mobile
- Easier 911 calling (tap = call)
- Navigation integrates native maps app
- Smaller modal fits phone screen
- Use "Get Directions" for turn-by-turn GPS

### Desktop
- Can contact multiple hospitals
- Better map overview
- Copy hospital phone numbers
- Print directions if needed

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety

**Your location data:**
- Only shared when you click "Find Hospitals"
- Transmitted securely (HTTPS encrypted)
- Not stored permanently
- Deleted after emergency resolved

**Hospitals receive:**
- Your location (latitude/longitude)
- Risk score and level
- Health summary
- NOT full medical records

## ⚡ Step-by-Step Emergency Usage

### Scenario: You Get Critical Alert

1. **Alert Appears**
   - See "CRITICAL HEALTH ALERT" modal
   - Risk score displayed (e.g., 82)
   - Three action buttons visible

2. **Find Hospital**
   - Click "📍 Find Nearby Hospitals"
   - Grant location permission when prompted
   - Wait for hospitals to load (2-5 seconds)
   - See list of nearest hospitals

3. **Choose Option A: Drive Yourself**
   - Click hospital name or "Get Directions"
   - Google Maps opens with directions
   - Follow turn-by-turn navigation
   - Arrive at hospital emergency room

4. **Choose Option B: Call Ambulance**
   - Click "📞 Call 911"
   - Explain your condition to dispatcher
   - Give address/location info
   - Stay on line until ambulance arrives

5. **Choose Option C: Already at Hospital**
   - Show list to someone to drive you
   - Call hospital directly
   - Check emergency wait times

## 🏥 Nearby Hospitals Information

### Typical Hospital Info Shown:
```
City General Hospital
Distance: 0.8 km away
Address: 123 Medical Center Dr, City, State 12345
Phone: (555) 123-4567
Specialties: Cardiology, Emergency, Surgery
Available Beds: 45
Rating: 4.8 stars
```

### How Distance Works:
- **0-1 km**: Walking distance (15-20 minutes)
- **1-2 km**: Close by (5-10 minute drive)
- **2-5 km**: Nearby (10-15 minute drive)
- **5+ km**: Further away (15+ minutes)

## 🚗 Choosing Best Hospital

### Consider These Factors:

1. **Distance** (Closest = Fastest)
2. **Specialties** (Matches your condition)
3. **Available Beds** (More beds = shorter wait)
4. **Rating** (Quality of care)

### Example Priority:
- Chest pain? → Look for Cardiology
- Trauma/accident? → Look for Trauma Center
- General illness? → Any with high rating

## 📞 Hospital Contact Information

### When to Use Each:

- **Use 911 Button** → Life-threatening emergency
- **Use Get Directions** → Can drive yourself
- **Use Hospital Phone** → Non-emergency, want to call ahead

## ❌ Closing Emergency Modal

You can close the modal by:
- Clicking the **X** button in top-right corner
- Only if you acknowledge the situation
- Emergency alert data is saved to your record

## 🔄 What Happens After Alert

1. **To You:**
   - Keep risk levels monitored
   - Follow doctor recommendations
   - Schedule follow-up appointments

2. **To Hospitals:**
   - Receive your emergency alert
   - Prepare care team
   - Alert emergency department
   - Prepare ICU if needed

3. **To Your Records:**
   - Emergency documented
   - Timestamp recorded
   - Location coordinates saved
   - Risk score history updated

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Will Finding Hospitals Cost Money?
**A:** No. The service is free to use. You only pay for actual medical services at the hospital.

### Q: Are My Location Details Private?
**A:** Yes. Your location is only shared with nearby hospitals during critical alerts and is deleted after resolution.

### Q: Can I Trigger Alert Manually?
**A:** Not from the dashboard. Alerts only trigger when risk score > 75. Contact support to report a false positive.

### Q: What if I Refuse Hospital Data?
**A:** You can use the system without sharing location. Call 911 directly instead.

### Q: Can I See What Hospitals Received?
**A:** Yes. Check your emergency history in your profile/settings.
