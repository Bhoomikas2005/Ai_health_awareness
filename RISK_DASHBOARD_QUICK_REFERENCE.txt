📊 WHAT IS THE RISK DASHBOARD?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A comprehensive health monitoring platform that provides:
✅ Real-time risk scoring (0-100)
✅ Disease predictions with confidence levels
✅ Health metrics tracking
✅ Risk trend analysis over time
✅ Personalized health recommendations
✅ Emergency alerts
✅ Historical data analysis

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🚀 HOW TO ACCESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

From Homepage:
1. Click "Features ▼" in navbar
2. Select "📊 Risk Dashboard"
3. View your comprehensive health assessment

Direct URL:
   http://localhost:5173/risk-dashboard

🎯 RISK LEVELS EXPLAINED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CRITICAL (Risk Score 80-100) 🔴
├─ Seek immediate medical attention
├─ Emergency services may be needed
├─ Urgent specialist consultation required
└─ High likelihood of serious health issues

HIGH (Risk Score 60-79) 🟠
├─ Consult healthcare professional urgently
├─ Schedule appointment within 24 hours
├─ Significant health concerns detected
└─ Immediate lifestyle modifications needed

MEDIUM (Risk Score 40-59) 🟡
├─ Schedule medical consultation
├─ Regular monitoring recommended
├─ Preventive measures important
└─ Monitor symptoms closely

LOW (Risk Score 0-39) 🟢
├─ Continue preventive care practices
├─ Maintain healthy lifestyle
├─ Regular check-ups recommended
└─ Monitor for any changes

📊 UNDERSTANDING RISK CATEGORIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Cardiovascular Risk (45%)
→ Heart and blood vessel health
→ Includes: Hypertension, Cholesterol, Arrhythmia
→ Recommendations: Exercise, Low-sodium diet

Respiratory Risk (35%)
→ Lung and breathing health
→ Includes: Asthma, COPD, Allergies
→ Recommendations: Air quality, Breathing exercises

Metabolic Risk (50%)
→ Metabolism and glucose regulation
→ Includes: Diabetes, Obesity, Thyroid issues
→ Recommendations: Diet, Weight management

Mental Health Risk (30%)
→ Psychological and emotional health
→ Includes: Stress, Anxiety, Depression
→ Recommendations: Counseling, Relaxation

Infection Risk (40%)
→ Immune system and infection susceptibility
→ Includes: Pneumonia, Flu, COVID-19
→ Recommendations: Vaccination, Hygiene

📋 API ENDPOINTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GET /api/risk-dashboard/analysis?period=week
└─ Fetch comprehensive risk analysis

GET /api/risk-dashboard/alerts
└─ Get current health alerts

GET /api/risk-dashboard/history?startDate=2024-01-01&endDate=2024-01-31
└─ Retrieve historical risk data

GET /api/risk-dashboard/recommendations?riskLevel=Medium
└─ Get personalized health recommendations

POST /api/risk-dashboard/compare (with startDate, endDate)
└─ Compare risk scores over period

POST /api/risk-dashboard/report (with userId, format)
└─ Generate comprehensive risk report
